Lesser Mending
Cantrip

- Repairs cracks, breaks, and tears if the pieces are held together
- Replicates or extends in minute amounts
- Inanimate objects, not bones or body parts
- Essentially it's a seamless adhesive
- Can be used to restore objects that have become brittle
- 


Greater Mending
Level 3 Spell

- Repairs bones, flesh etc
- Can be used by Wizards
- 1 minute cast time or more.



Superior Mending
Level 7 Spell

- Restores objects that have been mostly destroyed or burnt away
- Restores writing/inscriptions

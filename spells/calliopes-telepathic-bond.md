# Calliope's Telepathic Bond

Divination

Level: **3**
Casting time: **1 Minute**
Range: **Touch**
Components: **V, S, M (two tokens, personal to the bearers)**
Duration: **1 hour**

You perform a short ritual with one willing creature, using two tokens that are personal to the bearers. You create a telepathic bond between you and the target which lasts for 8 hours, as long as you both keep the others' token on their person.

Until the spell ends, the targets can communicate telepathically through the bond whether or not they have a common language, though the communication is only possible within 120 feet.

_**Spell Lists:**_ Bard, Wizard
